# appdoivo.com hub: ChatGPT design prompts (two new objects on the shelf)

Goal: the hub is one painted bookshelf in warm afternoon light; each app is an object on
the shelf (jar with a mossy cottage = Festa, star globe = Astro, typewriter and tape measure =
Job, glass dome with a sprout = more to come). Two apps join: **Journal** (a notebook) and
**Expenses** (a brass balance scale). The painting must stay the same painting: same light,
same objects, same places. Night mode is made by the page (dimmed + candle glow), so no
night picture is needed.

Attach `shelf-desk.webp` and `shelf-phone.webp` (in this folder) to prompt 1.
Use thinking mode. Send all prompts in the same chat so the style stays the same.

## 1. Two ideas for each object, on one board

this style and light, each on its own (no text):

Journal, a private diary with moods, notes, moodboards and photo albums:
J1. A cloth-bound journal, deep plum linen, tied with a thin ribbon, a pressed flower and the
    corner of a photo peeking out, standing slightly open on a small brass stand.
J2. A small stack of two notebooks under a brass paperweight, a ribbon bookmark and a dried
    flower tucked in, an instant photo leaning against them.

Expenses, a calm money diary, month by month:
E1. A small antique brass balance scale, a few old coins on one pan and a tiny folded paper
    receipt on the other.
E2. A little brass-cornered ledger, closed, with a coin purse and three old coins beside it.

Rules: the same warm afternoon light and painterly realism as the attached picture; each
object about the size of the typewriter; no letters, numbers or symbols anywhere (not on
coins, receipts, covers or pages); no modern things (no phones, cards or calculators).
